Session 5 - Not Trustworthy

Session 5 Image Log

Where You Left Off

You woke at the Iron Bend with Hollis finally at rest, a job from Neris still hanging unanswered, and a stolen box that needed to go back where it came from.

What Happened

Breakfast came with a confession.

Celienne told you where she came from — a life of music, stories, and entertaining, and a brothel she eventually bought her own way out of. Madame Martrill sent her off with a ring set with a pink stone. Nobody knows yet what it does, including her.

Then to business. The ledgers Neris wants are at the Licensing Office in the third tier. One file out, one file swapped, and goods quietly redirected toward Tallwood.

But first, the box.

You'd already opened it in the alley outside Kerra's estate. Pip already took an impression of the key inside. What you hadn't done was put the box back — and the longer it sat in your possession, the worse it looked.

So you split up. Celienne would go up to the estate alone and put it back. The rest of you would go to the Hearthleaf and keep Kerra occupied.

Celienne sent word ahead:

"Lady Kara, the Ladies League would like to meet with you."

The reply came cool: what is this regarding? You tried again, framing it around keeping her trade routes safe.

"That is a key goal of mine."

Tea at one o'clock. Enough time, if nothing went wrong.

Something went wrong.

Cutting up through South Market, someone shouted a name across the crowd:

"Quickgrin!"

Pip's name. Her real one.

"Thought you'd wash up somewhere with money in it. Boss said you'd run smart. Guess not."

No time to talk. Three of them, two up on a ledge, one behind a crate, and a market full of people who had come out for bread.

Elodie put the leader down with a single hit. Dhar held the front with the greatsword and dropped two of them. Athwyn's fire bolt sailed off into the sky, but her entangle later ended the fight without another body. Celienne tried twice to put one of them on the floor laughing, and settled for talking the last one into surrendering.

Then two more arrived — and made it very clear what this was:

"Give it up. We just want Pip."

They shot her in the chest. A thrown dagger put her on the ground.

Elodie's cure wounds brought her back. Pip's answer was to run straight up a market post, vault it, backflip off the top, and land on her feet.

"I'm alive, bitches!"

Five attackers, all taken alive. Nobody at this table killed anyone.

The city guard arrived as it ended.

You told them the truth, plainly — you were attacked, you defended yourselves, you subdued them. Bystanders backed you up. One of them added the part you'd left out: the attackers were only ever after Pip.

The guard took all five into custody. You mentioned a meeting with Kerra Thornkeeper, and were let on your way.

Walking on, Pip told you the rest.

They were Salt Ledger — the syndicate she used to run for, out of Greywash. They'd hired her to steal a ledger back for them. She took it, and instead of handing it over, she burned it.

That book held names, payments, bribes. Corrupt officials, paid guards, smugglers, killings dressed up as accidents. The people the syndicate quietly owns.

Pip never read a word of it. They will never believe that.

So they want three things from her: her silence, the name of whoever they think she was really working for, and anything that survived the fire.

While all that happened, Celienne went up the hill alone.

She found the kitchen door unlocked, slipped in, picked up a dust rag, and put on Ellyn's face. Ellyn herself was in the tea room, cleaning. So Celienne waited — twenty minutes in a nearby room while a girl she was pretending to be dusted a table.

When the room was empty, the box went back exactly where it had been, tipped and leaning as though something had knocked it there by accident.

On the way out, Ellyn looked up and saw someone walking away who looked a great deal like her.

Celienne made an alley, dropped the disguise, and sent word:

"The box has been placed. Positioned like we discussed. All went well."

You reached the Hearthleaf forty-five minutes later than you'd hoped but still on time for tea.

Kerra arrived without hurry and sat like the room belonged to her. Her tea was in front of her before she asked for it.

"So why have you summoned me here? What questions do you have?"

She asked where Celienne was. You said bad food at the Iron Bend. She didn't believe a word of it, and said she was sorry to hear it anyway.

Then you asked her, finally, what all of this was for.

"The needs come and go as time passes. I don't have one specific job for you — which is why I needed a team of people who were available."

"However, they need to be a trustworthy group. So the tasks I've been having you do were part of figuring that out."

You told her trust runs both ways. That being paid isn't the same as being trusted.

"Agreed."

Then she raised a hand toward Rowan, and four guards walked in.

"I'm afraid I've decided you are not trustworthy."

You asked why. She didn't answer. She didn't have to.

The charge is murder — Rowan was there when a Split Ledger operative accused you of killing Hollis, and Rowan works for Kerra. There will be a trial. Kerra explained how trials work here the last time you sat with her.

She never once mentioned the box.

You chose not to fight. You walked out under guard, and were taken down to the holding cells in the Switchback. When they searched you, they took everything — including the impression of Kerra's key, still in Pip's pocket.

Mid-transport, Celienne's voice arrived in your heads: how'd it go?

Arrested. Murder. Being taken to the holding cells. Lay low, and stay disguised.

"Message received. I'll do what I can for you guys."

What You Learned

Salt Ledger is not a Greywash problem anymore. They are here, they know Pip's name, and they want her breathing.

Kerra was never offering you a job. She was auditioning you for a place on a shelf, and she has decided you don't belong on it.

Rowan answers to Kerra. She always did.

Where You Stand Now

Four of you are in a cell in the Switchback, charged with murder, with everything you were carrying in an evidence box somewhere.

  • Celienne is still out there — free, disguised, and the only one of you who isn't behind a door.
  • The key impression is no longer yours.
  • Five Salt Ledger attackers went into custody today too.
  • Neris is still waiting on an answer about the Licensing Office. She has not been told anything.

The questions for next time:

Who speaks for you at a trial in a city where the person accusing you sits on the council?

How much can one bard do alone?

And why, exactly, did Kerra decide today of all days that you couldn't be trusted?
