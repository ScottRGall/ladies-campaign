Session 4 - A Seat at Every Table

Session 4 Image Log

Where You Left Off

You woke at the Hearthleaf with two pieces of business waiting: Kerra's invitation to tea, and the reply Elodie had been waiting on since she wrote to Maelin.

What Happened

You went to the shrine before the tea.

At the Shrine of the Low Flame, a quiet priestess walked you through more of how Halvyr actually holds together — the Council's role, the shape of the city's tiers, and a few pointed hints about the Split Ledger you keep hearing whispered about.

Elodie learned something bigger. Her circlet isn't just a symbol — the priestess confirmed it's a relic that actively judges the worthiness of whoever wears it. How much she tells the group about this knowledge is up to her.

Dhar tried, twice, to snuff out the sacred flame. Twice it refused to go out. Nobody has an explanation yet.

Tea at Thornkeeper Manor went smoothly — the linen-covered chairs joke from your arrival paid off all over again once you sat down.

You spoke with Ellyn while she served — she was clearly uneasy holding a real conversation while working, but you pulled a little more out of her, and out of Kerra directly, about the Council and about the Salt Ledger.

The moment you were clear of the estate, in the alley outside, Pip pulled out an ornate box — lifted from the tea room without anyone in that house noticing.

Inside: a single key. No note. Nothing that says what it opens.

You took a wax impression on the spot and decided the box goes back — better it's never known to be missing at all.

Afterward you collected your wagon from the Hearthleaf and headed for the Iron Bend.

Brannik gave you his read on the city from behind the bar — the same thing you've now heard more than once:

Halvyr eventually makes you choose a side.

You found Marrick, and through him, Neris Holt.

Neris didn't waste words. Prove yourselves, or don't bother coming back. Two things on the table:

  1. Recover Hollis's body and return him to his mother.
  2. A second job, only half-explained — something at the Licensing Office. Steal one file. Alter another.

You took the first job on the spot. The second is still just an idea hanging in the air.

Recovering Hollis was harder than expected — pulling him free of the pit, Dhar dislocated his arm, which broke the tension for a second in an otherwise grim job.

You brought him home to his mother. After a real goodbye, he was given a proper funeral pyre, dock custom.

Whatever this city cost him, that part is closed now.

You went back to the Iron Bend, told Marrick it was done, and called it a night there.

What You Learned

Halvyr keeps telling you the same thing from every direction: pick a side.

Elodie's circlet is watching her, not just marking her.

Neris doesn't do trust on faith — she wants proof, twice.

Where You Stand Now

You're resting at the Iron Bend for the night.

  • Hollis is home and at rest. That thread is closed.
  • The Licensing Office job is still sitting open — Neris is waiting to see if you'll come back for it.
  • You still have Kerra's box, and a wax impression of the key that was inside it. The box needs to go back before anyone at the estate notices it's gone.
  • Elodie's circlet is a bigger question than it was yesterday.
  • You've now heard, from three different people in three different rooms, that you can't stay neutral in this city forever.

Tomorrow's questions:

Do you go back for the Licensing Office job, or let it lapse?

What does it actually mean for a relic to judge you?

How much longer can you keep a foot in every camp?
